# Concentrated stock cubes

Reduce the layered_stock (open pot or under pressure), freeze it in ice cube trays, and dilute a cube in water when you need stock back. Takes far less freezer space.

- **Write the reduction ratio** on the tray or container. If you reduced 1.5 L to 150 ml (10:1), a cube isn't "add 100 ml" by default: the dilution is proportional. Without the label you'll guess wrong in one direction or the other.
- **Don't salt before reducing.** A salted stock reduced 10x becomes inedible. Salt only the final dish, after diluting. That keeps the cubes usable in any recipe.
- If the stock carries potato starch, strain it well before reducing: heavily reduced, the starch can clump when diluted.
- Freezer bank idea: one cube base of carrot, onion and garlic, and a separate one built on bacon and linguiça fat.

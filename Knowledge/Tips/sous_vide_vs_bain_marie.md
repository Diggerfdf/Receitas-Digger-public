# Sous vide is bain-marie (banho-maria) with control

Same physics: the food (or the yogurt jar, or the starter) sits in hot water instead of over direct heat. The difference is who controls the temperature.

- **Banho-maria:** the water is whatever temperature the flame gives you that day, and it can drift 5 to 10 °C with the burner and evaporation.
- **Sous vide:** the circulator (anova_nano) holds the water at an exact number, with a pump that moves the water so there are no cold spots.
- Everything already done in banho-maria (pudim, brigadeiro, melted chocolate, warm sauces) gets better: set the temperature once (e.g. 83 °C for pudim) and walk away, instead of babysitting the flame so the water never boils and splits the custard.
- Same idea for soups: the "low" setting on most stoves is still a simmer. Real temperature control stops vegetables from cooking down to mush.

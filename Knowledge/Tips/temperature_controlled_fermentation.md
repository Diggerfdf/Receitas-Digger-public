# Temperature-controlled fermentation

A chamber with a stable temperature doesn't care whether it's lactobacilli or yeast: it works for yogurt and for boosting a starter.

- **Starter / levain:** covered jar in a water bath at 24 to 28 °C. More consistent than a towel near the oven.
- **Yogurt:** yogurt makers typically run 42 to 46 °C. For long ferments (8 to 36 h), a circulator like the anova_nano holds any range.
- Put the sealed jar in the bath, not loose dough in the water.
- To separate whey from yogurt you don't need an accessory: diy_whey_strainer.

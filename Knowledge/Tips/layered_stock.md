# Layered stock

Instead of throwing out the cooking water, cook one vegetable after another in the same water. By the end it's a compound stock, and it replaces plain water in the soup.

**Order**

- Aromatics first (onion, garlic, celery): they're the base and benefit from the longest time.
- Then carrot: sweet and mild, only improves with more cooking.
- Potato last and briefly: the starch leaches fast, clouds the stock and makes it gluey. Pull it before it falls apart.
- Bitter or strong things (celery leaf, cabbage, broccoli stems): last, or in small amounts. Long cooking concentrates bitterness.

**Watch-outs**

- Each round loses some water to evaporation: top up with a little fresh water between vegetables.
- Don't salt the stock water. Salt only the final dish (see concentrated_stock_cubes).
- With a circulator (anova_nano) you can cook carrot and potato in separate sealed bags in the same bath at the same time: no ordering, no starch in the water.

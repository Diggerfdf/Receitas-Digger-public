# Right water temperature for tea and coffee

Water at 100 °C poured straight onto tea leaves or ground coffee scorches the aromatic compounds. With a circulator (anova_nano) in a pot, the water sits at whatever temperature you pick, as long as you want, and you just pour.

- Green and white tea: 70 to 80 °C.
- Pour-over coffee: 90 to 96 °C.
- Your pick for tea: 72 °C.

Ranges as quoted in the thread; adjust to taste.

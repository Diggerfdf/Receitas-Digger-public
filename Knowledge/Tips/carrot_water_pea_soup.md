# Carrot water in the pea soup

**Untested** idea: a version of the sopa_de_ervilha without bell pepper, using carrot instead. The original recipe is not changed.

**Plan**

- Cook more carrot than you need in the 1.5 L of water.
- Pull some of the diced carrot out early, still firm, and set it aside (the "crunchies").
- Let the rest keep cooking in the water.
- Use that carrot water instead of plain water in the pressure cooker with the peas. Whatever breaks down under pressure becomes body and flavor.
- Make the tempero (bacon, linguiça, garlic, onion) as in the original recipe, and stir the reserved cubes in at serving time.

**What to expect**

The bell pepper gave a sharp vegetal note against the pork fat. Carrot makes the soup sweeter and rounder: a different personality, not a worse one.

**Open:** amount of carrot, timing, and whether the water needs topping up before pressure. Fill in after testing, no guessing.
